Where the numbers come from

Prices are the latest regular-session trade (after-hours and pre-market when you ask for them, for example “nvidia after hours”) from Yahoo Finance, or from Nasdaq when Yahoo is busy, and from the Moscow Exchange for Moscow shares. They are fetched again whenever the page opens or comes back into view, and so are the r/wallstreetbets counts; sectors, industries and market values come from Nasdaq’s stock screener, read again every few minutes, and market values move with each minute’s price in between. Prices refresh about once a minute and can lag the exchange by 15 minutes or more. Each share is quoted in its own trading currency. The heat map’s slider compares the price now with the close that many days ago. “Since 1 Jan” compares with the last close of the previous year; “12 months” with the close a year ago, and is blank for shares listed more recently. Prices are adjusted for share splits. The 52-week range and the 60-day line use daily closing prices. “Basket” figures give every share equal weight.
